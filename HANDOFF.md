Treat this as a strong signal for the interpreted-vs-compiled decision, not a closed case — it's one VM's evidence, not a guarantee across builds/policy/cloud reputation state.
- Full test suite reconfirmed green after all of the above: 304 passed, 0 failed, 1 skipped, across multiple reruns.
- **Realistic captured installer**: the human operator ran `Start-NSPInstallerCapture` interactively against a real, official Notepad++ v8.9.8 installer (this part genuinely needs a human — hotkeys over a live GUI, no way around it). Two real findings came out of implementing that capture as AutoIt, both written up in detail in [docs/TestVM.md](docs/TestVM.md): the capture tool needs Tab-to-focus rather than mouse-hover before the hotkey (hovering never sets focus, so a first attempt captured the wrong controls); and this NSIS installer's buttons need `[ID:n]` selectors (from the capture's own `AutomationId` field) rather than `[TEXT:...]` selectors, because their real Win32 text carries a mnemonic ampersand (`&Next >`) that a naive text match won't find. Real conclusion, not a bug: a GUI installer wizard's natural home is **user-context** Intune deployment, not SYSTEM context — consistent with why IME uses silent installer switches for SYSTEM-context apps.
